## SCIENCE (2.42)

**SCIENCE** (app 2.42, bridge 1.34, D50; Timothy confirmed the mockup and six postulates 2026-10-24): one station `science` as the hub of a new SCIENCE group (to grow later; a LISTENING POST is next once his antenna is up), opening on SKY TRAFFIC. 🔭 Science Log in Notion (`NOTION_SCIENCE_DATABASE`; Event title, When date, Department and Kind selects, Detail, Value, Starred, Key; created by Claude with his approval). Location is `place()` rounded to 0.01° (`sciPlace`), as for the weather. Feeds: straight from the iPad (`sciGet`: SWPC Kp, Kp forecast, flares, F10.7; USGS; NWS alerts; Open-Meteo air quality; NeoWs with DEMO_KEY; iNaturalist; wheretheiss.at; adsbdb) and through the bridge (`sky`, `sciencefeeds`: no CORS there). Each feed on its own clock in `sc.d` (`tos.sci.v1`, `sciRun(k, ms, work)`, an error waits 5 min); aircraft every 10 s by `sciStart`'s timer, only while the station is open and visible. Away from the station only a light read (`sciLoad(false)`: orbits 12 h, Kp forecast 3 h, quakes 1 h) for the Bridge line and the briefing. Orbits and the sky are worked out on the iPad with the vendored satellite.js 5.0.0 and Astronomy Engine 2.1.19 (`js/vendor/`, MIT, lazy-loaded by `sciLibs`, precached by sw.js): `sciPassesFor` (30 s steps, ≥ 10°, sun below −6°, the station outside a cylindrical shadow), `sciNight`. Events: `sciDetect(k)` → `sciEvent` (`tos.scilog.v1`, 30 days, `sciTrim`) → `tos.sciq.v1` → `sciFlush` (25 per `sciencelogadd`, stars via `sciencestar` once the page id is known). Keys are stable (`ac:hex:day`, `pass:sat:dayThhmm`, `kp:time`, `flare:begin`, `eq:usgsid`, `nws:id`, `moon:full:day`, `met:name:year`, `neo:id:day`, `bird:subid:species`) so nothing is logged twice. Bridge line `sciLine()` after `inboxLine()` (`sciHeadline`: a ≥ 40° station pass still to come today, aurora when the Kp forecast reaches `sciAuroraKp(lat)` (NOAA's North American view line, roughly), an M4 within 300 km in 24 h, a meteor peak); never a Condition item; `tos.scilineoff.v1` turns it off. Briefing: `sciBrief()` adds a `SKY:` paragraph to the context (BRIEF_RULES 1.34). ASTROMETRICS row `science` (with `sciencelog`): events per week and by department; starred events join `asKeyDates` as "★ title". **Landmarks** (2.43, Timothy's ask; privacy first): `lm` state, `tos.scilm.v1` `{home, asked, at, list[{id, name, kind town / airport / own, lat, lon, rwy, auto, hidden}]}` and `tos.scilmoff.v1` (both in BK_KEYS). Suggested once by `lmSuggest` (Overpass, two mirrors, POST form so no preflight; the rounded location only) through `lmPick` (main airport and one more, then the biggest town per octant, one per ring band first, ≥ 6 km apart, ≤ 8, nothing inside 2 km, private fields dropped, runway heading from `ref`); SUGGEST AGAIN keeps manual ones, renames and hides. `lmSvg` draws them under the aircraft with label collision against the ring labels; `sciNear(a)` (≤ 8 km, flat-earth km from `sciXY`) adds "near …" to rows and a NEAR line to the card. Editor `lmEditor()` in Systems (Open-Meteo geocoding within 50 km, lat/lon, geolocation). Never write real landmark names or the centre's name into the repo or tests, and never put them in events, the bridge or Notion. SKY TRAFFIC repaints in place (2.44.2): `sciPaintSky` refreshes `#sciScopeBox`, `#sciLegend`, `#sciRows`, `#sciSrc` and only the card's `.sci-dbody` when the same aircraft and photo are open (`data-hex`, `data-photo`); `renderScience` moves an open `.sci-photo` node into the new page. The photo is `url_photo` before `url_photo_thumbnail`. Scope tap rings use class `tap`, not `.hit` (that opens the charts' tooltips and swallowed plane taps before 2.43). Tests: `bridge_science.test.js`, `sci_sky.test.js` (a real ISS orbit, checked against wheretheiss.at), `mock_v24` `scimode` / `scistats`, `app_e2e53` (every public feed stubbed with `ctx.route`).

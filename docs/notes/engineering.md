**Requests with a time limit** (2.89.1; after a power cut on 18 Nov every card showed "…" and SCENES "Checking…" while SAVE AND TEST passed). `haFetch` had no time limit; `haLoad` starts no read while `eng.inflight`, `scnLoad` none while `S.busy`, `scnCheck` none while `S.chk`, and `haLoad` repaints only when every device has answered. So a request that never answers (power cut, iPadOS pausing the app) blanked ENGINEERING for good, and requests left open also hold the browser's six connections to the relay, so later ones queue behind them (found by e95). **Vacuums** (2.92, D84; Timothy's Roborock, mockup approved 26 Nov, postulates 1 to 9): `vacuum` is in `HA_SHOW`; `capsOf` sets `vac`, `styleOf`/`bestStyle` give `"vacuum"` (no style choice, `stylePickHtml` says so), `haKind` `"vacuum"` → section CLEANING (`phead(..., "ok")`) in `.ec-side` between CLIMATE and SENSORS, `haVacHtml(d)` (CSS `ec-vac`, `ec-v*`; LIST view uses `haRowHtml`, whose text `haStateTxt` gives as "DOCKED · 84%").

## Morning Briefing (2.36, bridge 1.29)

`briefMaybe()` (from `tick`, `refresh`, `renderBridge`) compiles once a day: the first time Bridge is in front (not `document.hidden`; Standby counts, so a docked iPad compiles at 06:00) at or after `BRIEF_FROM` (06:00), when today has no briefing. It waits up to a minute for the facts (`briefReady`: the Bridge's 14-day events, today's tasks, weather if a location is set), fetching them itself (`briefFetch`) when another screen is open, re-checking every 3 s. `briefCompile()` sends `briefing(true)` without the parts in `BRIEF_SKIP` (tarot, apiary: Timothy, 2.36.1; so of the SHARE WITH ASK switches only finances can reach it; `ignoreList` applies) and the user turn `BRIEF_ASK` (about 80 words, no YESTERDAY / APIARY / TAROT) to POST `ask` mode `brief`: Sonnet (`AI.SUMMARY`), low effort, no tools, `BRIEF_RULES` asking for JSON `{headline, paras[{label, text}], action}`; logged as `brief`. `briefParse()` takes the outermost `{…}` (fences allowed), normalises labels and drops any "Condition …" part of the headline (Timothy: the folded line never repeats the level). Stored in `tos.brief.v1` (`day, at, model, cost, typed, fold`); a failure with no briefing today sets `br.err` (COMPILE NOW, automatic retry after 10 min), a failure on REFRESH keeps today's and toasts. Panel (`briefPanel`) sits above `conditionBanner`: COMPILING (a checklist ticking every 380 ms over the real wait), the paper, or the folded line. Printing: once (`typed`), 5 s, its own rAF loop that finds `#brPaper` by id so Bridge redraws don't restart it; a `pointerdown` on the paper finishes it (not click: the text under the finger is replaced every frame). Not while Standby covers it. Folding: `fold` true (−), false (opened, or asked for after 10:30 by REFRESH / COMPILE NOW / COMPUTER), null (auto at `BRIEF_FOLD` 10:30); another day's briefing is folded unless `br.showOld`. READ IT TO ME queues every paragraph in the tap (iPadOS needs the gesture), `onstart` lights each; the voice is `BRIEF_VOICE` (Isha, Premium preferred, via `briefVoice()`; the iPad's default when missing). Timothy heard the default in 2.36.1: Apple's forums say Web Speech offers only preinstalled voices, not downloads, and his iPad confirmed it (69 voices, no Isha). Systems shows `briefVoiceLine()`: the voice in use, or every English voice as `data-vtest` audition buttons (novelty `speech.synthesis` / `eloquence` voices last; nothing stored). He names one; set `BRIEF_VOICE` to it. Timothy wants no voice picker for now: ask before adding one. The AI voice is OpenAI's Nova (below); ElevenLabs Voice Design was the alternative discussed. Standby shows `.sb-brief` while today's is unread; the wake goes to the Bridge. Off switch `tos.briefoff.v1` (in backups). Without the `brief` capability the Bridge shows nothing and Systems names bridge 1.29. Tests: `bridge_ask` (mode brief), `mock_v24` `briefmode` / `briefstats` (capability off by default so other tests are unchanged; 1.2 s delay), `app_e2e47`.

**AI voice (2.37, bridge 1.30):** Timothy chose OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-tts, voice Nova, his style line in `VOICE.STYLE` (bridge). ## ON THIS DAY (2.81, bridge 1.48, D80)

`js/src/33-onthisday.js`; `otdPanel()` is appended to `renderBridge()` after `.rb-cols` and calls `loadOtd()` (needs the `onthisday` capability). One `onthisday` read for 5 years, kept in `tos.otd.v1` for the day (re-asked after 30 min when the bridge reported `problems`, so a partial answer heals). `otdWeather()` then asks Open-Meteo's archive (`archive-api.open-meteo.com`, inside CSP's `*.open-meteo.com`) once per shown year date with `place()`'s rounded coordinates and keeps `{hi, lo, rain}` (or null) in the same store.
- **Order and fallback:** `otdItems(y)` puts the exact date first in `OTD_ORDER`; a year with nothing on the date uses the week (±3 days) and is marked THAT WEEK; habits count only on the exact date. Years with nothing are dropped; no years, no panel.
- **Sheet:** `otdOpen(i)` fills `#detailSheet` (class `otd-sheet`) with year tabs (`[data-otd]`), the weather, each group and the privacy note.
- **The Log:** `go()` locks LOG whenever its screen is entered or left (90-app-shell), so `logOpen()` is never true on the Bridge and nothing of the Log is shown. Each sheet has OPEN THIS DAY IN LOG (2.81.1, `[data-otdlog]`, on every year alike so it says nothing about an entry): `go("log")` then `lg.want = date`; the PIN pad shows THEN OPENS, `logUnlock` opens `lg.want` instead of today, and `logFresh()` clears it when LOG locks.

## COMPUTER (2.35)

A command bar (Timothy's pick from three mockups, D43): `#cmdBtn` in the header, ⌘K / Ctrl+K (iPadOS keeps ⌘Space for Spotlight), or a swipe down on `.side` while it stands as a column (touch events; the click that ends the swipe is swallowed). `cmdItems()` builds the catalog each time: every station (`cmdStation`, with `CMD_WORDS` synonyms), actions gated by capability, and data rows (books, hives with New hive check / True up stack, the 78 cards via `trName`, unfinished `plays`, upkeep items, Systems settings via `sysJump(heading)`). `cmdSearch()` ranks title-prefix, then word-start, then title-contains, then anywhere (STATION before ACTION before data before SETTING). Empty state (`cmdEmpty`): WAITING ON YOU from `conditions()` mapped by `cmdCond` to what a Bridge tap does, RECENT and NOT OPENED LATELY (unpinned, 14 days) from `tos.visits.v1` (`cmdSeen` in `go()`; `_since` marks the first run so nothing is "quiet" on day one; not in backups). Rows only open things: an action is `go()` plus the station's own opener (`openAddBook`, `trGo`, `openPlan`, `cmdClick('[data-apact="trueup"]')`…), never a write. `lg.after` ("find" / "ask") runs once LOG unlocks. Opening loads Library and Apiary if stale (`cmdFresh` repaints). Matching is local only. Test: `app_e2e46` (tarot-apiary lane, :8105).

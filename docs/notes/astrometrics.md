## ASTROMETRICS (2.40)

**ASTROMETRICS** (app 2.40, bridge 1.32, D48; Timothy confirmed all six postulates of the mockup): station `astrometrics` (REFLECT, not pinned). Weeks are Monday to Sunday, week 0 the one holding 1 January (`asW0`, `asWk` in UTC day numbers, `asCols` 53 or 54). `asLoad(y, force)` runs each part through `asRun` (`as.busy` / `as.err` keyed `y:part`, render on landing, `shipMaybe()` when the last lands): `cal` via `asCal` (events eight weeks a chunk, `asBreak(ev) || !ignored(ev)`, `unionHours` per area in `AS_AREAS` order work, farm, personal, hobby; weeks through last week kept, the current week asked again), the `year` read (tasks by `taskArea`, queue buys), `habits` (year range; med days per week, walks, water goal days via `waterGoal`, longest med run), `reviews` (limit 104), `library` (lb.data if fresh), `tarot` (year range; card counts, majors), `apiary` (ap.data if fresh; honey, peak mites). Stored per year in `tos.astro.v1` (not in backups); stale after 6 h, or for past years until read after the year ended (`asStale`). Local rows: UPKEEP from `u.hist` (`asUpkeepDone` on mark-done, 2.40 on), LOG from `as.log` only: `UNLOCK LOG` sets `lg.after = "astro"`, `logUnlock` calls `asFromLog()` which copies `lg.dates` before `go()` locks the log; `as.log` clears on `visibilitychange` hidden. Never put the Log days in storage, the Ship's Year context or any request. `asRow` gives values (null before the first record or after this week), `asPeak` (not for `flat` rows REVIEW and LIBRARY), `asLevel` against the row's own max. Screen: `shipPanel`, `asTiles`, `asChart` (CSS grid, sticky row names, `asPlace` draws TODAY and scrolls a narrow chart to it once; redraws keep the scroll), `asReadout`, `asSector` (`asBars` SVG with `rect.hitm` taps, `asFacts`), `asFoot` (RESCAN, what needs 1.32). The pager steps years (`asYear`); THIS YEAR shows for past years. Ship's Year: `shipContext(y)` (counts only; tarot with `tos.aitarot.v1`, apiary with `tos.aiapiary.v1`, the queue's spend with `tos.aifin.v1`; never the Log), POST `ask` mode `year` (`YEAR_RULES`, Sonnet, low effort, no tools, JSON like the briefing, parsed by `briefParse`, drawn by `briefSegs` / `briefHtml(segs, n, say)`), stored in `tos.shipyear.v1` (in backups) by year with `final`; `shipMaybe()` (tick, refresh) compiles the whole year at the first open after 06:00 on 31 December. Nova via `shipVoice` (IndexedDB key `year`), Moira via `shipSay`. Tests: `bridge_year.test.js`, `bridge_ask` (mode year), `mock_v24` `astromode` / `astrostats` (capability off by default), `app_e2e51`.

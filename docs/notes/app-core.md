## Keeping the page still (2.62.1)

Timothy saw LOG jump while typing (top in portrait, bottom in landscape) and HOLODECK's TV move on CH ▲▼. Two rules from it: (1) **never measure an element by collapsing it** (`height = "auto"` then `scrollHeight`) while it's on screen: the page shrinks for that moment and the browser clamps the scroll (portrait: the page itself scrolls, `.content` is `overflow: visible` under 860 px; landscape: `#content`). `growLog()` measures `logMirror` (a hidden `textarea.cl-mirror`, absolute, the editor's width, beside it); `growBox()` keeps every scrolling ancestor's position instead (and skips `.cl-mirror`). (2) **A redraw that replaces `#content` must put the scroll back**: `holo.render()` and `tgPaint()` save `window.scrollY` and `#content.scrollTop` first and call `keepPlace()` after (instant, not smooth). Tests: `app_e2e76` (LOG; fails on the old code at 820 × 1180), `app_e2e77` (HOLODECK; fails on the old code on the first CH ▲ in portrait). Both run at 820 × 1180 and 1180 × 820 with touch.

## The error log (2.62)

Timothy: "the last 30 calls list moves quickly and if failures occur they disappear". `errLog(src, what, msg, detail)` appends to `errlog` / `tos.errlog.v1` (LEFT_OUT of backups, like `tos.netday.v1`): `{t, s: bridge|holodeck|audio|page|rules|voice, a, m, d, v (app version), n, l (last seen), rt (retried)}`, 200 lines, 30 days; the same `s`/`a`/`m` within 10 minutes among the last 10 lines adds to `n` and moves to the end. **Everything passes `errScrub()`** (`k=`/`key=`/`pin=`/`token=`/`code=`/`auth=` values, `Bearer …`, any run of 32+ key-like characters), and nothing else may be written there: action names, codes, browser messages, a playback file's base name. Never put data (task titles, Log text, names) into an `errLog` call. Sources: `logged()` (every failed bridge attempt, `err.errEntry`; `markRetried` bumps `rt`; `netDay()` counts the day's requests, failures and slowest), `window` `error` and `unhandledrejection` (skipped when the reason carries `errEntry`/`netEntry`), the CSP listener, AUDIO's `pl.el` error, HOLODECK's `hdErr()` (file, channel, LEVEL's state). SYSTEMS: `errSection()` after `netSection()` (`#sec-errlog`, chips `data-elf` only with 2+ sources, 25 lines then `errall`, `errcopy` = `errReport()` to the clipboard, `errclear` twice within 4 s), repainted in place by `errPaint()` (300 ms after a new line while SYSTEMS is open); `errClick` is called from the main click chain. COMPUTER: "Error log". Test: `app_e2e75`.

## Requests and the page's rules (2.58, bridge 1.38)

**A quiet wake (2.81.2, D81).** `wakeUntil` is set for `WAKE_MS` (60 s) whenever the page becomes visible or `online` fires. In that window `withRetry` treats a dropped request (TypeError) or `navigator.onLine === false` as the network reconnecting: it waits `WAKE_WAITS` (1.5, 3, 6, 12, 20 s, or until `online`) and tries again, and `unlogAttempt(err)` takes the attempt out of the error log and today's failure count, leaving it in NETWORK as `dropped · waking`. Timeouts are untouched. Before this, each wake left a burst of "dropped · Load failed · 0.0 s" in the error log (VPN reconnecting after sleep). Note: the error log keeps a failed first attempt even when its retry succeeds ("1 retried" with no second entry means the retry went through).

- **Every bridge request is a POST** with a text/plain JSON body `{key, action, …}` (no preflight). Reads go through `apiOnce` → `postOnce` with `readBody(params)` (values as text, as a URL carried them). `readVia[url]` learns per bridge address: an `unknown_action` to a first read by POST means an older bridge, so reads go by GET (`apiGetOnce`) unless that GET doesn't know it either; a GET answered `use_post` switches back to POST. Bridge `doGet` answers `use_post` to anything with a key, and `no_key` to an empty GET (a POST whose body a redirect dropped: `transient()` retries it). `doPost` takes `ping`, `batch` and everything `read_()` knows after the writes. Bridge tests call `doPost({ postData: { contents: JSON.stringify(params) } })`.
- **Simulated bridges:** `tests/mock_post_reads.py` wraps every `mock_v*.py` (`wrap(H)` before `serve_forever`): reads by POST answered through the mock's own GET code; GET `?action=readmode&post=0|1&get=0|1` simulates an older bridge or 1.38's refusal. Tests that intercept bridge requests read the action from the POST body (`app_e2e10`, `12`, `14`). Test: `app_e2e71`.
- **Content-Security-Policy** is a `<meta>` in `index.html` (GitHub Pages can't send headers): `script-src 'self'` (no inline scripts or handlers, ever), `style-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'` (style attributes), and an explicit `connect-src` / `media-src` list. **A new outside service needs its host added there**, or the browser blocks it; `127.0.0.1` and `localhost` are for the tests; `*.ts.net` is the NAS relay (and Home Assistant behind it). A violation is noted in RECENT REQUESTS (`securitypolicyviolation` listener → `note()`, "blocked · host"). Before a release that adds a host, a sweep that throws on a violation and runs the suite is cheap (2.58 found none).

## Service worker

**Service worker updates** (2.43.2): `sw.js` no longer calls `skipWaiting()` on install (a new version taking over mid-reload left a header over a black screen or unstyled HTML on the iPad); it installs (`cache: "reload"` requests, past the HTTP cache) and waits. UPDATE READY posts `"skipWaiting"` to the waiting worker (after `reg.update()`, retried once, and waiting for `installed`), reloads on `controllerchange`, 15 s at most. Closing and reopening the app activates it too. Keep it this way; `app_e2e54` publishes two versions to a copy of the app on :8125 and checks each comes up whole. Since 2.50 a navigation answered with an error (a host 500 or 404) falls back to the cached app page instead of showing or caching the error; only same-origin GETs are cached (`app_e2e54` runs a server that can be told to fail).

- `sw.js` caches only the app's own page as the offline copy; other pages on the site pass through.

## Storage: a full store is never silent (app 2.77, D73)

`js/src/01-core.js`. `lsSet` used to swallow the quota error. Now a write that doesn't fit calls `lsMakeRoom`: it drops `LS_COPIES` (pure copies a sync refills), largest first, then `LS_REMADE` (lengths, tags, artwork: measured again), retrying after each. Still no room: the text goes in `store.failed[k]`, `lsGet(k)` answers from it (so read-modify-write code keeps working and queues still flush from memory), `lsRetry` runs every 30 s, on `pagehide` and from MAKE ROOM AND SAVE, and `lsTell` toasts once on filling and once on clearing. `lsFull()` (the failed keys) is the first Condition item (`lvl: "bad"`, fix `store`; `fixStore` → `storeClear(true)`). A copy or an `LS_QUIET` key (netlog, netday, errlog, visits, sync) that doesn't fit is only noted in `store.skipped`: it never evicts anything and never alarms. `storeSection()` (SYSTEMS, `#sec-store`, after backup) shows `lsUsage()` (characters × 2 as MB, "of about 5 MB": Safari's limit; Chrome allows twice that, which is why e86 fills the store itself instead of assuming a size) and CLEAR SAVED COPIES. `lsName` gives keys readable names (`LS_NAMES`). **Rules:** a new key a sync fully refills goes in `LS_COPIES` (and `LEFT_OUT.cache`); never put there anything holding what a sync can't bring back (`tos.scilog.v1` holds unsent events, `tos.brief.v1` a paid briefing, `tos.sci.v1` de-duplication); `backup_keys.test.js` checks the lists. Never call `localStorage.setItem` directly for app data. Test e86.

## Motion and standby

Motion is gated so background syncs never animate: `enterScreen()` adds `enter` + `grow-in` to `#content` only from `go()` and `page()`; other effects are classes added after a user-triggered render (`turn`, `opening`, `justdone`, `changed`) or `easeHeight()` / `slideFrom()`. Sheets open and close through `showSheet()` / `hideSheet()` (200 ms slide before `hidden`). The pointerdown `flash` gives every button the LCARS light-up. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns all of it off (CSS rule plus `calm()`).

Standby: `#standby` overlay over everything. `checkIdle()` every 15 s against `tos.standby.v1` (0, 5, 15 default, 30 minutes; skipped while typing). `STANDBY` in the top bar (`#idleBtn`) and Systems → STANDBY NOW enter it directly. The wake tap is handled on the overlay's click, so nothing underneath is pressed. Entering fades in over 1.6 s; waking (`.waking`, 900 ms ease-out, `SB_WAKE_MS`) redraws the screen underneath first, then hides the layer only on `transitionend` (fallback timer): never hide it on a shorter timer than the fade, which is what made 2.10.1 and earlier "pop" halfway. `app_e2e21` samples the wake fade frame by frame, manual and automatic. Night look 22:00 to 06:00 dims the text, never the black layer. Drift of a few pixels each minute for OLED. Screen Wake Lock is requested while standby is on (re-requested on return and on the next tap). Tests: `app_e2e21` (uses `clock.install` for the timeout).

**Arm press** (2.14.1): pressing `.elbow` restarts `.flash` on the elbow and `.topbar` and `.armflash` on `.top` (its `::before` curve) in the same tick; never brighten or tint the corner alone (no `:active` filter on it), or the join shows. `app_e2e24` checks the three filters match mid-flash.
